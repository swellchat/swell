# Swell Messenger — Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

Draft for legal review. Not yet published. Publisher: PT JIJI LABS DIGITAL ("Jiji Labs"), Indonesia.

## Summary

Swell is designed so that Jiji Labs does not receive your identity, contacts or messages.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, IP address, or the content or metadata of your messages. Swell has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

## What stays on your device

Your identity keys, contacts, messages and settings are stored encrypted on your device. You can delete them at any time with **Settings → Delete identity**.

## How messages travel

Messages are end-to-end encrypted and sent over the Tor network directly to your contact's device. If your contact is offline and you both enabled a mailbox, the encrypted message is held by the mailbox for up to 14 days, then deleted. The mailbox cannot read messages and does not know who sent or receives them. The mailbox keeps no access logs.

## Voice calls

Call audio is end-to-end encrypted and passes through two relay servers run by different operators. Your relay sees your IP address and the call's start, end and data volume, but not who you call or what is said. Relays keep no logs after a call ends. In private call mode, audio goes over Tor and no relay is used.

## Reports

If you report a contact, the messages you select and that contact's safety number are sent to Jiji Labs. We use reports only to review abuse and meet legal duties, and delete them after 90 days unless the law requires longer.

## Requests from authorities

We can only provide what we hold: reports users chose to send. We hold no user accounts, contact lists, IP addresses or message logs.

## Children

Swell is for people aged 18 and over.
