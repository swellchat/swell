# Swell Messenger — Child Safety Standards (DRAFT)

Publisher: PT JIJI LABS DIGITAL. To be published at `jijilabsdigital.com/swell/child-safety`.

1. Swell prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and sextortion. Such use ends access to support and is reported to authorities.
2. Swell is for users aged 18 and over.
3. **In-app reporting:** every chat has *Report*, which sends the selected messages and the contact's safety number to Jiji Labs. Every chat also has *Block*.
4. **Action on CSAM:** when we gain actual knowledge of CSAM through a report, we preserve the report, act on it, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the competent regional authority, including Indonesian law enforcement.
5. **Design limits:** messages are end-to-end encrypted and are not stored on our servers, so we act on what users report.
6. **Child safety contact:** childsafety@jijilabsdigital.com.
