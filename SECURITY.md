# Security Policy

Swell has had an internal security review and has not been independently audited.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Open a private report at https://github.com/swellchat/swell/security/advisories/new, or email support@jijilabsdigital.com with the subject `Swell security`. Please do not open a public issue. Policy and Hall of Fame: https://getswell.org/security

We aim to reply within 7 days and to publish a fix and advisory within 90 days. Reporters are credited unless they ask not to be.

## Scope

Protocol design, the Android app, the mailbox and build reproducibility. Out of scope: compromised devices, global network observers, and third-party services (Tor network, Google Play).
